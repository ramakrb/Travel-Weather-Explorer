How this works

Search & query. Type any words — country, visa situation, notes, weather ("diving", "visa-free", "typhoon", "whales"). Use the month picker to ask "where is best in March?" — every destination is re-ranked 1–12 for that month (weather + crowds) and sorted #1 first. Region chips, advisory filter and cost sorting narrow things further. Click any card for the full 12-month picture.

Monthly weather. Typical low–high temperature ranges for the main tourist areas, from long-term climate normals, rounded. Big or regionally-split countries carry split notes in the month cells (e.g. coast vs mountains, west vs east, north vs south).

Ranked months (1 = best, 12 = worst). Each month's rank weighs temperature, rainfall and humidity, seasonal hazards (typhoons, hurricanes, cyclones, extreme heat) and crowds, festivals and price peaks. Your workbook's own "Best month(s)" pick stays visible in every card, and ranks 1–2 always fall inside it. For split-climate countries (Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Vietnam, Brazil, South Africa…) the rank is a national compromise — read the month notes for the regional truth.

Sources. Advisory levels, visa rules, costs and notes are carried over from your workbook, where they were verified November 12, 2026 against travel.state.gov and official entry-rule sites. Weather values are approximate climate normals, not forecasts. Levels, visa policies and fees change — recheck before booking.

Map. Country boundaries from Natural Earth Data. Click any country to see its best travel month and a quick climate snapshot.

Precipitation. Average monthly rainfall (mm) from long-term climate normals. Lower values generally indicate better travel conditions.

Travel Types. Tags indicate ideal trip styles. Filter by type to find matching destinations.

Favorites. Click the heart icon to save destinations. Your favorites persist in browser storage.

Export. Download your filtered results as JSON/CSV or share a link that restores your view.
